Understand the response

A clearer picture of your room.

01The arc and the ring
The outer arc follows reported state and temperature response. The fine ring shows measured temperature slope, with readable direction, rate and units.
02A chip with context
Heating, Cooling, Idle, a VT heat request or reported valve opening: the chip follows the observations available. Tap to see the source behind the status.
03A small LED, a fuller picture
Green signals readiness; amber highlights restrictions or incomplete observations; red marks disconnection or an unavailable thermostat. Tap for provider details and request status.
04Detail when you want it
The chevron reveals supported modes, presets and provider features. Top-row readings open focused panels. The everyday face stays simple.

TRV means thermostatic radiator valve. These installation examples show how valve position and temperature trend can vary independently.

Choose your controls

Your readings, arranged your way.

Choose top-row readings, their order, labels and icons for each card. Window, presence and motion have clear states. Humidity follows your display bands, and supported presets keep their own colours.

Make it your own

Choose Midnight Blue, Plum or Black. Set humidity display bands and the details that matter to each room. Save these per-card preferences in Home Assistant’s visual editor.

Work with your thermostat

Start with a standard climate entity or Versatile Thermostat. Add humidity, trend and valve sources independently. Verified administrators can open VT lock controls when the provider’s capabilities are available.

Each reading keeps its source and status. Optional response mappings combine configured room, trend and output evidence.

Additional sensor and telemetry sources are optional and depend on the integrations you use. Outdoor/weather entries are mapped readings and conditions; control policy stays with the configured provider. Independent sensor readings can reach TI without passing through VT.
