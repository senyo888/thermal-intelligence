Designed to be read

Every detail has a purpose.

01The arc and the ring
The outer arc reflects reported state and temperature response. The fine ring around the sphere shows the measured temperature slope. They can tell different stories at the same time.
02A chip with context
Heating, Cooling, Idle, Heat requested, or a reported TRV opening: the chip follows the evidence available. Tap it to see the source behind the status.
03A small LED, a fuller picture
Green signals readiness; amber highlights restrictions or incomplete observations; red marks disconnection or an unavailable thermostat. Tap for temperatures, provider details and request status.
04Detail when you want it
The chevron opens supported modes, presets and relevant features. Top-row readings open focused panels. The main face stays simple.

Anonymised installation examples. A reported valve position is not proof of heat delivery. TRV means thermostatic radiator valve.

How the arc colours work

With VT response sources configured, orange requires a below-target room, an accepted current heat request and fresh room/trend readings that agree it is warming. Blue below target means warming is not yet established. Reported cooling is cyan, the target band is green, above target is lavender, and Off or unavailable states are muted.

The slope ring independently follows rising, falling or near-flat readings. The activity chip reports the thermostat or valve state; Idle does not impose a universal arc colour. Without response mappings, the card retains its temperature-comparison and reported-cooling colours.

Make room for what matters

Your readings. Your setup.

Choose the top-row readings, their order, labels and icons for each card. Window, presence and motion distinguish Open/Closed, Home/Away and Detected/Clear. Humidity follows your display bands; supported presets keep their own colours.

Humidity, presented your way

Set four boundaries for too dry, your good range and too wet. The colours are local display preferences; they do not change heating or humidity control. Save them in Home Assistant’s visual card editor.

Controls that follow your thermostat

Start with a standard climate entity or Versatile Thermostat. Add humidity, trend and valve sources independently. Verified administrators can open the VT Lock/Unlock dialog when its capabilities are available.

0.1.0-beta.4

Ready to try it?

Beta.4 removes a false amber warning when VT reports no optional Off or Mode reason. Genuine unknown, unavailable and malformed observations remain visible. Command permissions are unchanged.

A separate beta card

Install the exact module from senyo888-patch-1 and select Thermal Intelligence Thermostat · Beta in Add Card. Keep existing TI resources and cards in place.

No TI backend is required. HACS installation is not yet supported, and there is no stable release.

Clear testing limits

Checks cover HA 2026.10.0 and 2026.10.4 with VT 10.5.1. Manual installation and test-environment rollback have been exercised. Physical heating, native phone/tablet use and a minimum supported version range remain unverified.
